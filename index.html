03

Research

M.Sc. thesis · EIDOSLAB · 2023

Deep Learning on Histopathological Images for Cancer Grading

Pathologists grade colorectal cancer from histological slides by looking at tissue differentiation — an assessment that is inherently subjective. At the same time, abnormal microRNA expression is known to drive the cancer's initiation and progression.

I trained convolutional neural networks to extract compact, meaningful representations of tumour tissue from gigapixel whole-slide images, then built a secondary multimodal model to explore how those image features relate to each patient's miRNA profile.

Supervised by Prof. Francesca Cordero, Prof. Marco Grangetto and Dr. Carlo Alberto Barbano.
